# Third-party material

## AtCoder problem library

Problem identifiers, Japanese statements, available official English statements, examples, and illustrations originate from [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/). Each local statement record retains its specific source URL. Original content remains the property of its respective rightsholders. Chinese statements and the 15 APG4bPython English statements are independent translations for this practice library, not official AtCoder translations.

The software license does not grant rights to redistribute AtCoder material. Review applicable permissions before publishing the bundled problem content in a public repository or distributing it commercially.

## AtCoder Problems

The bundled metadata snapshot comes from [AtCoder Problems](https://kenkoooo.com/atcoder/) on 2026-10-24. Its [source repository](https://github.com/kenkoooo/AtCoderProblems) documents the API and project license. Estimated difficulty and official contest point values are different metrics.

## Pyodide

Pyodide 0.27.8 is bundled in `vendor/pyodide`. It includes CPython and other runtime components. See the included license files and `checksums.json`. [Pyodide source](https://github.com/pyodide/pyodide).

## MathJax

The retained local MathJax distribution is bundled in `vendor/mathjax`; its included LICENSE governs that distribution. [MathJax source](https://github.com/mathjax/MathJax).

## CodeMirror

The local Python editor uses CodeMirror 6 and Lezer. Bundled dependency notices and MIT licenses are in `vendor/editor-LICENSES.txt`; exact build dependencies are recorded in `package-lock.json`. [CodeMirror source](https://github.com/codemirror/dev/).
